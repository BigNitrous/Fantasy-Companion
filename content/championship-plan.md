The sample is small. They were not rebuilt on Nov 8. Schedules and the cold-outdoor tags come from that same working file (ESPN scoreboard at the time). Projected points were not used then, and this update does not add them.

The 5-3 record, 84% odds, and 4th-place standing are the Nov 3 dashboard snapshot. Opponent sketches use that pass's roster notes and, where you had already played them, the score. They are not standings.

Matchup strength on the weekly dashboard is only what ESPN and Sleeper already bake in. This plan's ranks are a separate, older chart. Neither one is a promise about December.
