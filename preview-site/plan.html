Where this stands

You decide

This is an advisory plan for Deshaun, Don't Touchdown there in The Big Leagues (ESPN, 12-team PPR). It lays out a case, a ranked order, and a week-by-week checklist. It does not set your lineup, send an offer, or drop a player. The weekly dashboard is the live record, the optimizer, and this week's win chance.

The plan was rebuilt on Nov 8, 2026. Where that update conflicts with the Nov 3 pass, Nov 8 wins. Two changes drive the rest of the page.

  • C.J. Stroud is the starting quarterback going forward. He is not a trade chip.
  • Patrick Mahomes is the quarterback to move. The priority landing spot is Isaiah Likely.

The Nov 3 record was 5-3, 4th of 12, with 84% playoff odds on the dashboard. That snapshot is not refreshed here. After Week 9, read the record on the weekly dashboard.

Top actions

  1. Keep Stroud. Offers that send him are superseded. The NFL deadline on Tue Nov 10, 3:00 PM CT still matters for real roster news, especially Jonathan Taylor. It is not a reason to move Stroud.
  2. If you want a tight end upgrade, talk to BTAstfu about Isaiah Likely (NYG). He is open to a medium-to-high deal. The ranked offers are below. Mahomes can be in one live offer. The plan puts Likely first and advises against shopping Mahomes to several teams at once.
  3. Keep the core out of those offers: Jonathan Taylor, Josh Jacobs, Chuba Hubbard, Matthew Golden, and Carnell Tate. Tre Tucker stays out too: his chart value sits above Likely.
  4. Do not offer A.J. Brown straight up for Likely. Brown is 22.8 on the chart. Likely is 13.0.
  5. Plan the bye weeks off the roster you have now, not the Nov 3 roster. Week 11 still takes Jacobs, Golden, and Brown. Week 13 still takes Taylor, Tucker, and Washington.

Likely offers

BTAstfu is open

The Nov 3 note that BTAstfu would not sell Isaiah Likely is superseded. As of Nov 8 he is open to trading Likely for medium-to-high value. Current sources list Likely on the Giants (NYG).

Ranked offers, and only these. The order is the plan's advice. You send what you decide to send.

  1. Ceiling: Mahomes + Kelce for Likely. Add nothing more. Do not sweeten it with Brown, Meyers, Tucker, Hubbard, Golden, Tate, Taylor, or Jacobs.

  3. Reject: Mahomes + Meyers. The plan advises against sending it.

Do not offer A.J. Brown straight up for Likely.
